What the audit supports

C4’s direct Table 2 row is mixed. TT is 4.63% (MISMATCH), observed validation-with-validation is 1.58% (MISMATCH), and validation-with-train is 4.60% (MATCH). A separate uniform fixed-size split expectation is 1.60% (MISMATCH), with an expected validation count of 5,849.19.

Wiki-40B needs both evaluation arms. TT is 0.70% versus 0.39%. EE matches 0.26% under validation and test; ET is 0.70% under validation but 0.72% under test. The printed label valid is not renamed.

Table 11 is an exact-ID diagnostic. Exact full-ID misses do not reconstruct or contradict the source-native host-level aggregation. C4’s promised twentieth printed row remains SOURCE_MISSING; RealNews has zero exact full IDs eligible for its derived ranking.

LM1B remains printed-only. No LM1B cluster archive is pinned. Its conditional printed-only removal floor is 510,877, exceeding Table 13’s largest printed-difference-compatible value by 70,877.
